Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about using NumberHub.

NumberHub is an online platform that provides virtual phone numbers for receiving SMS verification codes for supported services.
Choose Buy Numbers, select the available country and service you need, choose an available number, and complete the purchase using your NumberHub wallet balance.
After purchasing a number, use the SMS/OTP option on your active order to check for incoming verification messages.
The active period depends on the number provider and the specific number purchased. Please check the details shown for your order.
Give the service some time to send the message and use the SMS/OTP option to check again. If the SMS still does not arrive, contact NumberHub support for assistance.
Open Fund Wallet from your dashboard and follow the available payment instructions to add funds to your NumberHub wallet.
If a purchase fails after your wallet has been charged, the transaction should be reviewed and any eligible refund will be handled according to NumberHub's refund policy.
Open My Profile, scroll to Change Password, enter your current password and your new password, then tap Change Password.
You can contact NumberHub support through the Telegram Support option in the dashboard.
